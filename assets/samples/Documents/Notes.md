## Keyboard tips

1. `Super` opens the start menu; just start typing to search.
2. `Alt+Tab` switches windows, `Super+Left` and `Super+Right` snap them.
3. In the editor, `Ctrl+F` finds, `Ctrl+H` replaces and `Ctrl+/` comments.
